The **Highlighter mobile app** is an iOS app for the people doing inspections in
the field. It signs in to the same Highlighter account you use on the web, shows
you the cases you are working on, lets you talk to the rest of the team about
them, and gets the imagery you capture off the phone and into Highlighter.

Start with [Getting started on mobile](./getting-started-on-mobile/) to sign in
and register your device, then follow the guide for the job you are doing.
